>SiIvaGunner is a semi-anonymous musical collective founded by 'Chaze the Chat' in January 2016 as a parody of GilvaSunner, itself a tribute to the defunct SilvaGunner YouTube channel from 2008. SiIvaGunner specializes in bait-and-switch YouTube videos that appear to be "high quality rips" of video game music but instead deliver remixes, parodies, mashups, and meme-filled surprises, unlike GilvaSunner and SilvaGunner, which uploaded actual rips of video game music[.](https://www.last.fm/music/SiIvaGunner/+wiki)
