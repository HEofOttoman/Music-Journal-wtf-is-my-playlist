>"Akari" (燈), meaning "light" or "lantern,", hit J-Pop song by Japanese singer-songwriter Soushi Sakiyama. Widely famous as the first ending theme for Season 2 of the anime Jujutsu Kaisen, covering the Hidden Inventory/Premature Death arc.

Range: June

A beautifully fitting outro song. Described by some as 2000s nostalgia, it is.. very, nostalgic to say the least. The parting of best friends on a montage of shared memories.

To me, this song represents a dear moment. Wow, cringe, I know.
